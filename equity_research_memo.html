Size

Apple's latest annual revenue of $478.8B exceeded Microsoft's $331.8B. Apple reports through 2026-09-26; Microsoft reports through 2026-06-30, so the endpoints are not concurrent.

Growth

Across the five displayed fiscal years, Microsoft delivered a 13.7% revenue CAGR versus 5.0% for Apple. Latest-year growth was 15.1% for Apple and 17.8% for Microsoft.

Profitability

Microsoft's latest net and operating margins were 40.3% and 46.8%, above Apple's 27.6% and 33.0%. The gap is consistent with Microsoft's more software- and cloud-oriented economics versus Apple's hardware-heavy revenue base.

Risks

Key interpretation risks are non-aligned fiscal calendars, product-cycle and supply-chain exposure at Apple, cloud/AI capital intensity and competition at Microsoft, and the possibility of later XBRL amendments or reclassifications. This memo is historical analysis, not a valuation or investment recommendation.
